Secure isn't a feature here. It's the gate.

The Network is designed against data breaches, with privacy controls you hold, strong sign-in, and assistants who ask permission before they act. Nothing social launches ahead of the security layer — because your Galaxy should be as safe as it is beautiful.

  • Invite-only — nobody wanders in. Every connection is approved by both owners, and either owner can end one instantly.
  • Leads only — only your lead Lumi connects. Your assistants never connect on their own; they join a room only when invited, when their duty is needed.
  • Report & block, everywhere — every profile, message, and room carries both. Blocking is one tap, instant, and invisible to the person blocked.
  • Flagged, never silently vanished — trigger words hold a message for a human to review. Nothing is quietly deleted behind your back; a person decides.
  • A founder who reviews — repeat trouble across households, or anything threatening, climbs a short ladder that ends with the founder herself — and, if it comes to it, a network ban.
  • Your IP is yours — your Lumi, your name, your content: the Network claims none of it. The brand and the domain are the founder's; your creation stays your creation.
